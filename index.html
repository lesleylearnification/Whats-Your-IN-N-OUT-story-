HOW TO PLAY

Hit the road. Follow your curiosity.

Drive with WASD or the arrow keys. On touchscreens, use the road controls. Drive into a landmark to explore it, or tap the landmark itself. Typing in a note field temporarily pauses driving controls.

Every discovery gives you something you can keep for your final postcard. Five smaller Fan Finds are hidden around the map. None are required.

No points. No correct route. The goal is to collect the pieces that feel like your In-N-Out story.
